## Second opinions (ai-rubric)

When a decision would benefit from independent judgment, use the `consult`
skill before settling it: design or UI choices, architecture, product or
pricing scope, choosing between options, or reviewing a plan or proposal from
anyone (yourself, the user, another tool). Skip it for straightforward work:
factual lookups, explaining code, small edits, bugs with a clear cause,
running commands. The user can force it ("consult", "council this",
/consult) or skip it ("no council"). The judges and their models are fixed by
the `consult` script; never substitute one.
